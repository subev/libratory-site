Your privacy

Libratory Reader for iPhone

Libratory Reader collects no data. You do not need an account. The app has no code that connects to the internet.

Your books stay on your phone

Books you add are copied into the app. We never receive their pages, audio, or covers.

Your reading place, listening history, saved passages, notes, and groups stay on your phone, too.

No tracking

The app has no usage tracking, crash reporting, advertising IDs, or third-party software kits. We cannot see when you use it.

You choose what to share

Tap Share to send the original book file to an app or folder you choose. This does not include your reading place, saved passages, or notes. The app you send it to has its own privacy policy.

You can also export your saved passages as plain text to a place you choose.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Delete your data

Deleting the app removes its books, reading places, saved passages, and notes. We have no copy of them.

Changes

If a future version sends data, we will update this page before it is released.
