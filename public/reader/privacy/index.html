Libratory

Libratory Reader for iPhone

Privacy policy. It is short because there is nothing to disclose.

Libratory Reader collects nothing.

There is no account to make. There is no server to sign in to. The app contains no networking code at all — it cannot send anything anywhere, because there is nothing in it that knows how.

Your books stay on your phone

A book you import is copied into the app's own storage on the device. Its pages, its narration and the cover are never uploaded, never mirrored and never seen by us.

What you do with a book stays on your phone too

Where you stopped reading, which parts you have listened to, the passages you marked, the notes you wrote on them and the groups you filed them in are all stored on the device alongside the book. We never receive them.

Nothing is tracked

No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, no third-party software development kits of any kind. We do not know that you opened the app, and we could not find out.

What leaves the phone, and only when you ask

The app has a Share action. Choosing it hands the original file you imported to whatever you pick in the iOS share sheet — a message, another app, a folder. It shares that file only: your reading position, your marks and your notes are not included. Nothing is shared unless you tap it, and where it then goes is decided by the app you chose and governed by that app's own privacy policy, not this one.

Exporting your marks writes them out as plain text, to a destination you pick, in the same way.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes every book, position, mark and note with it. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to request and nothing to be forgotten.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything anywhere, this page will say so before that version ships.
