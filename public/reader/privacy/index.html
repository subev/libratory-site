Your privacy

Libratory Reader for iPhone and iPad · local reading and optional cloud processing

Local reading is free and does not require a Libratory account. Imported books, connected shelves, supplied narration and translations, and local voices remain available without a hosted subscription. Optional online page preparation, live translation and voices send selected content to the provider you choose, after permission. Hosted features require Sign in with Apple and the eligibility described below. This notice covers these features where offered, including test builds; availability depends on the installed version and whether cloud access is open.

Libratory is operated by Petar Sabev. For privacy requests, contact support@libratory.dev. Petar Sabev is the controller of the personal information described here. Please use private email, not a public issue, for account or personal information.

Your library stays on your device

Imported books, reading positions, saved passages, notes and local narration are stored on your device. Local reading does not upload a book to our cloud processing service. Device backups and files you share follow your device settings and the destination’s policies. Deleting your Libratory account does not delete these local books or their progress.

When content is sent

Approving a provider does not itself upload anything. Importing a book does not authorize a cloud upload. Content is sent when you use an online feature with that provider, including the limited preparation of upcoming passages needed for uninterrupted listening. Approving several providers does not send each request to all of them.

Depending on the feature, inputs can include page images, extracted text, nearby text needed for context, or translated text to be spoken. A page image or passage can contain personal information. The app identifies the recipient, purpose, information sent, and provider training and retention disclosures before you allow it. You can withdraw permission in account settings. Withdrawal stops new work; it cannot recall information already sent.

Our relay forwards reading content transiently. It does not keep a server library of your books, page images, prompts, generated translations, narration, audio or reading history. This is different from saying that no data is processed or that every provider retains nothing.

Cloud providers

Depending on availability and your choice, OpenAI, Anthropic or DeepSeek processes page images or text to prepare a readable passage, translate it, or match words between the original and translation. xAI or Inworld receives text to generate an online voice. A voice provider may receive translated text when you choose to listen to a translation.

Provider information: OpenAI API data controls, Anthropic commercial API training policy and retention policy, DeepSeek Open Platform terms, xAI API data handling, and Inworld privacy notice. API or enterprise processing may be governed by separate agreements; a provider's consumer-app privacy policy is not a promise about this service's API settings.

Only providers shown as available in the app can be selected. Availability depends on your region, age eligibility, permission and service capacity. The disclosure identifies the provider actually used; approving one provider does not approve another.

We do not promise zero retention or account-specific exclusion from provider training where those settings have not been verified. Treat content submitted under such a disclosure as potentially retained or reviewed according to the provider’s policies. The relay is hosted by Cloudflare; Apple supplies sign-in and purchase services. Their processing is separate from our transient handling of reading content. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

xAI (where offered): Zero Data Retention is enabled for the xAI team used by our service. See xAI’s API data-handling documentation and enterprise terms for the scope and conditions of this setting. This setting does not remove Libratory’s necessary account and credit records or change another provider’s retention policy.

DeepSeek (where offered): account-specific training exclusion and retention limits are unverified. Treat submitted content as potentially retained, reviewed or used under the DeepSeek Open Platform terms. Do not treat Libratory’s transient relay as a promise of no provider storage or training.

International processing and safeguards

Cloudflare, Apple and a selected provider may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. The destination and applicable safeguards depend on the recipient and service.

Processing can take place in the United States and other countries where our providers and their subprocessors operate. DeepSeek is based in China; its direct service may involve processing there. We do not promise that cloud processing stays within the EEA.

Published contractual safeguards are described in the data-processing addenda of Cloudflare, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI and Inworld. These documents include European Commission standard contractual clauses for covered international transfers, subject to their scope and conditions. The linked documents explain those safeguards and the providers’ subprocessor arrangements; contact support@libratory.dev to request information or a copy of safeguards applicable to our service.

DeepSeek direct API: we have not verified an equivalent data-processing agreement or contractual transfer safeguard covering Libratory users’ content. DeepSeek’s Open Platform terms distinguish its developer privacy policy from downstream users’ data. We do not represent that its consumer privacy policy, a provider permission switch or this notice establishes that coverage. This uncertainty is separate from the retention and training disclosure above.

Where a provider supports it, we send a stable pseudonymous safety identifier derived from your account and scoped to that provider and environment. It does not contain your name or email. It helps the provider investigate misuse; it is not a guarantee that one person’s activity cannot affect shared service availability. Provider support for this identifier varies by endpoint.

Account, purchase and service records

Cloud accounts use Sign in with Apple. We retain the identifiers and session information needed to authenticate you, your provider-permission decisions, and a reported age band with its source and policy version. We do not collect a birth date or identity-document image through this flow. A reported age band is not independently verified identity. Where required, Apple age and app-association checks also apply.

We keep numerical credit balances, grants, charges, reservations, usage estimates and corrections, opaque request references and verified Apple purchase/refund records. These records are associated with your account. Apple handles payment details; we do not receive your card number. A support reference locates your account but does not grant access to it. We do not send Apple consumption data in response to refund requests.

To identify service failures, we record limited cloud-blocker metadata such as provider, task category, fixed failure or recovery status, time and account reference. These records do not contain book text, titles, images or audio. We keep the latest state for each account, provider and task, including first and last failure and recovery times. These records are scheduled for deletion after 30 days without a new observation and removed on account closure. They help us explain restrictions and restore service.

Why we process information

We use account, session and transaction data to perform our service contract: sign you in, provide requested cloud work and administer credits and purchases. Consent is the basis for optional provider content processing and optional automatic failure-report sharing. You can withdraw those permissions without losing local reading.

Our legitimate interests in service security, preventing duplicate grants or charges, and resolving disputes support minimal operational records and the limited trial-claim record described below. Where a specific legal duty requires records, we retain only the records and period that duty requires.

Account identifiers, eligibility information and relevant provider permission are required for hosted work; without them, that work cannot proceed. Purchase evidence is needed to grant purchased credit. Automatic failure reports and support correspondence are optional. Shelf credentials are separate from your hosted account.

Automated checks use age and region eligibility, permissions, credit and service capacity, prior trial claims and purchase status to allow or refuse hosted work or new purchases. They can temporarily restrict cloud access; they do not stop local reading. Contact support for a human review of an incorrect restriction.

We do not use advertising identifiers or sell your reading information. Apple may provide TestFlight feedback and diagnostics under your Apple settings; this notice does not describe those as anonymous.

Optional failure reports

Where failure reporting is available, the app keeps the newest 16 local stage or model failures on your device for 24 hours. Each record contains only fixed fields: the stage, failure category and recovery status, the provider and model, an HTTP or relay status code, the duration, the app version and build, and opaque report and request references. It never contains book text, titles, files, pages, reading positions or audio, and successful reading is not recorded.

You can preview a report and copy it. An email draft containing it opens only when you choose to open one, and nothing is sent until you send that email. Optional automatic sharing is off by default; when you turn it on, reports are delivered only while the app is in the foreground. Turning it off stops future automatic delivery.

A report delivered while you are signed in is linked to your account. We keep the newest 20 such reports for up to 30 days and delete them when the account is closed. A report delivered while signed out carries no account or installation identifier and is kept for up to 7 days.

The service’s own failure observations described above remain automatic and do not depend on this setting. Our monitoring receives aggregate reliability, usage, credit and cost, and capacity metrics, with no account, request or book identifiers in these aggregate exports. Failure reports are separate from permission for hosted-provider processing; sharing a report does not grant permission to send reading content to a provider. A report you email becomes support correspondence and follows its separate retention.

Retention and deletion

Account and sign-in information is kept until account closure. Closing the account revokes access and deletes its active profile, sessions and permission records, with only narrowly necessary records retained separately as described here.

Credit, purchase, refund, trial-claim and necessary duplicate-request evidence is kept while the account is open, then for 24 months after closure, to resolve disputes and prevent repeated free trials or duplicate charges. The trial record includes a minimal protected identifier derived from your Apple sign-in identity; it remains personal information, not anonymous data. A documented legal obligation or active dispute may require a narrowly limited extension for the affected records; those records are removed when that need ends.

Signed-in failure reports are kept for up to 30 days and removed on closure; signed-out reports for up to 7 days. Service backups expire within 30 days; deletions are reapplied before restored data returns to service. Genuinely anonymous aggregate reliability, usage, credit and cost, and capacity metrics may be kept indefinitely; these aggregate exports contain no account, request or book identifiers. These backup limits concern our service, not your device backups or a provider’s separate retention.

Closure does not reset earlier spending or cancel an Apple subscription. It does not recall content already sent to a provider. Provider retention follows the applicable disclosure and policy.

You can request access, correction, deletion or a portable copy of your personal information where applicable, and request restriction or object where applicable, by emailing support@libratory.dev. Where processing relies on consent, withdrawal does not undo processing already performed. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority, including Bulgaria’s Commission for Personal Data Protection.

Private support

Support receives what you choose to send. An email opened from the account screen includes an account reference to help find your case; review the draft before sending. Do not include passwords, API keys or private book passages. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the case and related disputes or obligations.

Shelves, downloads and sharing

A shelf is operated by its owner. Pairing sends the phone name you choose and establishes a separate shelf credential. Requests list or download books and include app/iOS/device information. A paired shelf can associate downloads with that device. The public shelf issues no paired-device key and records download counts with the requested file, time, bounded user-agent information and country when supplied by its proxy. These are download records, not your reading position, notes or listening history.

Optional local voice packs download through GitHub or Hugging Face and their delivery services, which receive the network request and IP address needed to deliver them. These downloads do not contain your book; installed local voices process text on the device. Apple manages its own voice and language assets. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Hugging Face’s privacy policy.

Sharing sends the file or annotations you choose to another app or service. Search Web sends selected text to your chosen search destination. Source links open their destination. These actions follow those services’ policies and do not authorize unrelated cloud processing.

Age and availability

Local reading remains available without a cloud account. Hosted features require Sign in with Apple, age 16 or older, any higher minimum required by the selected provider, and lawful access in your territory. Hosted features are unavailable in mainland China. The app may require Apple age or app-association checks and any legally required guardian permission. Younger or otherwise ineligible readers can continue locally. The App Store content rating and permission to use a particular cloud provider are separate.

Changes

Material changes to cloud processing are reflected in this notice and the applicable in-app disclosure. Updated provider permissions may be requested before new processing. Contact us if a disclosure or restriction is unclear.
