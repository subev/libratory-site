Help with reading, cloud access and purchases

Email support@libratory.dev for private help from Libratory’s maintainer, Petar Sabev. Postal address: Izgrev, block 25, entrance 12, apartment 20, Burgas, Bulgaria. You do not need an active account to contact us.

Help us find the problem

Use the support action in the app’s account screen when possible. Its draft includes your account reference; you can inspect it before sending. Include your app version, approximate time, the provider or feature, and the error message. A screenshot is useful if it contains no private book text or personal information.

Where failure reporting is available, the app can also prepare a failure report from your newest recent failures. You can preview or copy it, or choose to open an email draft containing it. It holds fixed failure details, the provider and model, app version and opaque references, never book text, titles, files, pages, reading positions or audio. Optional automatic sharing is off by default and works only while the app is in the foreground. The privacy notice explains what a report contains and how long it is kept.

Never send your password, Apple authorization code, API keys or the book itself. An account reference helps locate a case; we may need separate proof of ownership before sharing private account details or making changes.

Free reading, trial access and invitations

Local reading, connected shelves, supplied narration and translations, and local voices stay free and account-optional. Hosted features require Sign in with Apple, age 16 or older (some providers require a higher age), lawful territory and permission for the selected provider. Hosted features are unavailable in mainland China.

Where cloud access is open, a free trial provides 100 credits once per Apple identity, subject to capacity. If trials are temporarily full, try again later; local reading remains available. Subscriptions (US$19.99/month for 600 credits) and top-ups (US$9.99 for 400 credits) are currently by invitation. The App Store’s local price is authoritative. Use the account screen’s support action to request access; please review its account reference before sending.

Credits or a purchase are missing

First use Check purchase status or Restore Purchases where offered. Do not buy another pack to resolve a pending purchase. TestFlight purchases are simulated; the separate funded beta allowance can run out even when test credits remain. Include your account or purchase reference when asking for help.

If a request’s actual cost remains unconfirmed, reserved credits are returned 24 hours after the provider first started. We carry later supplier costs and do not debit you later for that released request. If the hold remains, contact us with its reference and time. An explicit retry can incur another charge; do not retry merely to clear a pending balance.

Refunds, privacy and deletion

For an Apple cash refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com. Cancel future subscription renewals through Apple’s subscription settings. EU withdrawal rights and notice routes are explained in Apple’s applicable storefront terms, subject to applicable law; there is no unconditional 14-day refund promise for all purchases or renewals. For incorrect credit charges, delivery problems, privacy requests or deletion difficulties, email us privately. See our service terms and privacy notice. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Libratory support remains available for service complaints and statutory remedies, including after account closure. We do not send Apple consumption data for refund requests.
