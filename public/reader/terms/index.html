Free trial and purchases by invitation

Where cloud access is open, a free trial provides 100 credits, once per Apple identity, subject to eligibility and capacity. Trials can be temporarily full. The free trial does not start a paid subscription or charge you automatically.

Subscriptions and top-ups are currently by invitation. Contact support from the account screen to request access; an invitation is not guaranteed. Existing credit remains usable subject to normal service eligibility and capacity.

The subscription provides 600 credits for US$19.99 per month. A top-up provides 400 credits for US$9.99. The App Store’s purchase screen shows the authoritative price in your local currency, billing period and applicable terms before you confirm. Top-ups require an active subscription and a balance below 200 credits, including credits reserved for work in progress. We do not automatically buy top-ups.

The monthly subscription renews automatically and Apple charges your Apple account unless you cancel through Apple’s subscription settings. Cancel before the renewal date shown by Apple to stop the next renewal. Deleting Libratory or your Libratory account does not cancel the subscription. Previously purchased credits do not expire when a billing period or subscription ends; cancellation stops future renewals and their new credit grants.

Processing, compatibility and availability

Hosted work starts when you request an online feature with an eligible provider and sufficient credit, including bounded preparation of upcoming passages for listening. It needs internet access and a compatible app, device and OS. Available languages, document types, voices and features depend on the provider and the options shown in your installed app. Older supported systems may offer fewer features; check the App Store compatibility information and in-app availability before buying.

Credits pay for processing, not a fixed number of pages or minutes. Usage depends on the chosen provider and the amount of work. Network conditions, document complexity, provider availability and funded service capacity affect speed and access. We do not promise uninterrupted service or a fixed completion time. Contact support if paid service fails or a restriction is unclear; temporary unavailability does not waive your statutory remedies.

Usage, cancellation and unconfirmed cost

Cloud work reserves credits before it starts. Never-started work is free. After provider work starts, cancellation or a lost connection may not stop processing; confirmed usage is charged within the original reservation.

If the actual cost remains unconfirmed, reserved customer credits are returned 24 hours after the provider first started. A retry or disconnection does not restart that deadline. We carry any supplier cost confirmed later and never debit you later for that released request. Service recovery may delay when a returned balance appears; contact support if it remains pending.

Other work can continue using remaining available credit and capacity. An explicit retry is a separate task and can incur another charge. We do not automatically resend uncertain paid work. Contact support for incorrect charges or delivery failures; a credit correction is separate from a cash refund.

Cancellations, refunds and withdrawal rights

Purchases are made through Apple. Subscription cancellation stops future renewals; it is separate from requesting a refund or exercising a statutory right of withdrawal. For refunds, use Apple’s refund service. EU withdrawal requests follow the routes in Apple’s applicable storefront terms, including Purchase History and the notices described there, subject to applicable law.

There is no unconditional 14-day refund promise for every purchase or renewal: Apple’s terms distinguish the initial subscription from renewals and explain the digital-content exception after delivery is requested with the required acknowledgment. Nothing here removes any withdrawal right or other mandatory remedy that applies to you.

For a service problem or complaint, email support@libratory.dev. We investigate failures and incorrect charges and remain responsible for our service obligations. Depending on applicable law and the problem, statutory remedies may include bringing the service into conformity, a price reduction or termination and reimbursement. Apple’s billing route does not replace Libratory support.

We reconcile verified refunds, including partial refunds and reversals. An approved refund may remove unused credit or subscription access attributable to that purchase. We do not impose automatic cash debt or confiscate unrelated valid purchases. A refund request alone is not evidence of abuse. Evidence of payment abuse may pause new cloud spending for human review; contact support to challenge mistakes. We do not send Apple consumption data for refund requests.

Account deletion

Delete your account through the in-app account controls, or contact support if you cannot access them. Deletion ends cloud access; your local books and progress remain. Manage your Apple subscription separately. The privacy notice explains retained records and their deadlines. Contact support about unused credit or a purchase completed after deletion; you do not need an active account to ask for help.

TestFlight purchases

Purchases in TestFlight are simulated and do not charge you. Processing still costs real money, so test accounts have a separate funded allowance that simulated purchases cannot increase. Contact support if test credits remain but processing is unavailable.
