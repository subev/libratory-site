Add a shelf

A pairing link from the Libratory desktop app

This link adds a shelf to Libratory Reader on your iPhone or iPad. A shelf is the list of books the desktop app has made, so the phone can download them over your own network.

On a phone with the app

Open this link on the phone and it opens in Libratory Reader, which shows you the shelf and asks what to call your phone before anything is added. Scanning the code with the Camera app does the same.

You can also add a shelf from inside the app: open Shelves from the library, tap Scan a code, and point the camera at the code on the desktop app's Phone page.

Without the app

Get Libratory Reader, then scan the code again from the Phone page. Each code is used once, and the page makes a fresh one.

Get Libratory Reader

What this page knows

Nothing. The code and the address of your desktop app are in the part of the link after the #, which stays on your phone and is never sent to this site.

Libratory Reader is free, on the App Store. The desktop app is free and open source.
